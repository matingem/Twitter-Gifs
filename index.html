X GIF → WhatsApp Stickers

Paste an x.com / twitter.com link to a GIF or video. It gets converted to a 512×512 animated WebP.

0 stickers (WhatsApp needs at least 3)
  1. Install Sticker Maker (Android) or a similar app that opens .wastickers files.
  2. Tap Add to WhatsApp, choose that app in the share sheet (or open the downloaded file with it).
  3. In the app, press its “Add to WhatsApp” button.
Stickers are capped at 500 KB and 8 s. The converter lowers quality/fps until each one fits.
